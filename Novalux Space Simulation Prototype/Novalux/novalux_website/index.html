PLAYER HANDBOOK

How to Play NOVALUX

A practical walkthrough for first-time players, with device-specific controls and mission tips.

START HERE
FIRST MISSION · QUICK START

Your first mission, step by step

  1. Choose a destination.Select Earth, Moon, or Mars from the mission database.
  2. Design your spacecraft.Choose a platform, propulsion, power source, instrument, communications system, and launcher. Watch mass, power, and cost.
  3. Check the mission plan.Review orbit, launch capability, resource margins, and communications before proceeding.
  4. Launch and reach orbit.Ignite. Keep throttle on during ascent. At 70 km or higher, release the fairing. When a stage runs out of propellant, press Separate Stage. Repeat until the final stage separates; then wait for orbital-insertion verification and continue.
  5. Start Mission Gameplay.Use the device controls to navigate, avoid hazards, and collect science objects.
  6. Review your results.Open Science Results and Mission Reports to understand your performance and export evidence.

ADVANCED EXPLORATION

Vehicle and camera tips

  • Use the on-screen activity buttons or WASD / arrow keys to steer.
  • Rover: drive to each glowing sample beacon. When close, press E or tap SCAN / CHECKPOINT to collect it.
  • Docking: use W / S and A / D or the thrust buttons in short pulses. Aim for less than 0.5 m/s relative speed and within 20 m of the station.
  • Rotate your phone to landscape for launch, docking and rover play. In the rover 3D view, drag to orbit and use the mouse wheel to zoom; touch pinch zoom is supported where available.
  • Double-click the rover view to switch camera mode; press C to reset follow view.
MISSION STRATEGY

How to improve your score

  • Keep payload mass within the launch vehicle's modeled capacity.
  • Maintain a healthy power reserve and avoid unnecessary power demand.
  • Choose instruments that match the scientific objective.
  • Plan enough communications capacity to return collected data.
  • Read event messages before choosing an operational response.
NEED HELP?

Recommended learning path

Start with Guided Discovery in Mission Scenarios, then follow the first-mission steps above. The engineering and physics models are simplified for learning; use the in-app notes to understand assumptions and limitations.
